# PROTOCOL ZERO V0.2.7 — independent evidence fusion

## Base and scope

Base: research/v026-integrity-evidence at commit
0b2b4621cdbd68e7cad296f9bdcfab99660880aa.

V0.2.7 adds provenance-aware evidence fusion. It does not alter the V0.2.5
CRC solver or the V0.2.6 collision witness. It remains an offline analysis
library: no device commands, active probing, automatic repair, or real-device
validation are included.

## Implemented interfaces

protocol_zero.evidence provides:

- EvidenceSource: source identifier, declared independence group, source kind.
- BoundarySeries: complete externally measured boundary positions over an
  inclusive offset coverage interval. A missing boundary inside declared
  coverage is a contradiction; a position outside coverage is unknown.
- StructuralRule: frame-relative field constraints using an allowed-value set,
  inclusive numeric range, or equality to frame length minus a fixed delta.
- FrameCandidate: candidate span and CRC-model vote counts.
- candidates_from_decode: adapter for joint_crc.decode agreed and contested
  spans, preserving partial votes.
- evaluate_candidate / fuse_candidates: provenance-aware evaluation.

Evidence groups, not raw evidence-item counts, are aggregated. Multiple
structural rules from one protocol document count as one provenance group.
CRC and constraints derived from the same capture can be assigned the same
group; they then cannot inflate the independent-support count.

## Decision policy

- corroborated: at least two distinct declared provenance groups support the
  candidate, with no group-level contradiction.
- single_source_supported: exactly one provenance group supports it.
- conflicted: at least one group contradicts it, even if other groups support.
- partial: only partial support is available.
- unresolved: no support or contradiction is available.

This is a qualitative evidence ledger, not a weighted score or probability
model. A source group that contains both support and contradiction is treated
as contradicted. Every result keeps integrity_verified false.

The word independent refers only to user-declared provenance groups.
The software cannot prove that two instruments, documents, or derivations are
statistically independent, authentic, or correctly calibrated.

## Reproducible synthetic mechanism test

CRC-8 parameters: polynomial 0x07, init 0x00, xorout 0x00.

- Prefix: AA 55 03 07 10
- Original payload: ASCII PAYLOAD
- Deleted byte: 0x44 (ASCII D)
- Original CRC: 0x4E
- Padding byte: 0x78
- Observed stream: 00 00 AA 55 03 07 10 50 41 59 4C 4F 41 4E 78 FF
- CRC-valid alternative candidate span: [2, 15)
- Independently observed UART boundary: offset 14

The alternative span includes the shifted original CRC as payload and the
padding byte as its candidate CRC. It passes the CRC model and all three
protocol-spec constraints (sync, payload length, message type). The external
boundary source contradicts the candidate end. Result: conflicted.

The result records two supporting provenance groups (capture CRC and protocol
specification) and one contradicting group (logic-analyzer boundary). It does
not auto-repair the stream or certify the intended frame.

This is a deterministic synthetic mechanism test. It demonstrates that the
fusion logic can surface a contradiction in a constructed case; it is not a
sensitivity, specificity, false-acceptance, or real-traffic performance claim.

## Limitations and next experiment

BoundarySeries is valid only when its source really enumerates all boundaries
inside the declared coverage. If the instrument can miss edges, its absence
must not be encoded as a contradiction; narrow the coverage or treat the
source as partial. The current data type requires complete coverage semantics.

Structural rules are deliberately small and frame-relative. There is no
schema language, nested-field parser, bit-field support, timestamp alignment,
or authenticated evidence channel. Provenance is user-supplied metadata.

Next, evaluate the same capture under controlled loss, duplication, jitter,
and boundary-detector miss rates. Compare CRC-only, CRC-plus-structure, and
CRC-plus-independent-boundary fusion on fresh holdout seeds, and report
candidate rejection, unresolved rate, false acceptance, and missed valid
frames separately. Do not tune on the holdout set.
